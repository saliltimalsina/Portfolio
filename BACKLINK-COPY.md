## Pro tip: Anchor text matters

When you can choose the visible link text, prefer:
- "Salil Timalsina" — for the name itself
- "Salil Timalsina portfolio"
- "UI/UX designer Salil Timalsina"

Avoid: bare URLs only, or generic "click here". Google reads anchor text
as a vote for what the destination is about.
